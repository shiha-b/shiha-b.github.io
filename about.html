About Me

Electrical and Electronic Engineer specializing in UAV systems, robotics, and embedded flight control systems.

My Passion & Expertise

I am a recent graduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering with a deep obsession for all things that fly—drones, planes, UAVs, and anything that takes to the skies. My specialization lies in designing and developing flight controllers, robotics systems, and embedded solutions for autonomous vehicles.

My thesis focused on creating a low-cost dual-core flight controller for quadcopters and testing its performance under partial propeller disturbance conditions. I'm passionate about making aerial robotics accessible and affordable while maintaining high performance and reliability.

Beyond technical work, I've helped establish and lead the Robotics Club at KUET, mentoring over 100 students in hardware and robotics fundamentals. I believe in sharing knowledge and inspiring the next generation of engineers to explore the exciting world of robotics and UAV technology.
